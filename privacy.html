JeBlock V0.2.3 Privacy

This PWA does not create an account, run analytics, or upload JeBlock settings.

The Maximum beta profile uses a third-party public encrypted DNS resolver with OISD and Apple-native filtering for testing. DNS queries are therefore processed by that resolver operator under its own privacy policy.

The production plan is to move JeBlock to JeBlock-controlled DNS endpoints with minimal retention.
