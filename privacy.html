JezBlock V0.1 Privacy

This PWA does not create an account, run analytics, or upload your JezBlock settings.

Your selected profile and optional custom DNS-over-HTTPS URL are stored locally in your browser.

Important: when you use a third-party DNS resolver, DNS queries are processed by that resolver according to its own privacy policy. V0.1 uses AdGuard Public DNS for the bundled Standard and Family profiles.
